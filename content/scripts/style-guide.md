# Style guide for the video scripts

Borrowed from Chris Raroque's channel (https://www.youtube.com/@raroque). Taken from his titles, descriptions and chapter lists only. I haven't watched the videos or read transcripts, so this is structure to borrow, not a voice to copy.

- State the challenge first, then a brief self-intro, then the rules.
- Honest hooks. Admit mistakes and reversals.
- Show the downsides and say who it's for.
- Process over polish. Talk to the camera like a friend. Use the script as a list of beats, not lines to read.
- Candid titles for process posts: "This simple feature almost broke my app" style.
- Short vertical format here. His are 8-15 minute videos, so borrow structure, not length.

## Weekly post pattern

- Tuesday: the app. Hook (what it does and who it's for), show it working, one honest limit, where to find it.
- Thursday: the process. One real thing that went wrong, shown as problem, cause, fix. Or one prompt next to its result. Or the test that failed first.
