# Advent Calendar Builder: scripts for two posts

Filming: Fri Nov 27. Edit Mon Nov 30. Posts: Tue Dec 1 (the app) and Thu Dec 3 (the process). See `style-guide.md`.

Use made-up messages on camera, never ones written for a real person. Turn on Do Not Disturb first. For filming, set the calendar's start date in the past so door 1 opens on camera.

Rule: show only what exists. Record the build sessions on Tue-Thu without sound, so the process post has footage.

## Post 1: the app (Tue Dec 1), about 45 seconds

Week 1 of the challenge. Hook, show it, one honest limit, where to find it.

| # | Time | Picture | On-screen text | Voiceover (a guide to the beats, say it your way) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0-4 s | A door opening on the phone, a message appears | "Week 1: advent calendar" | "Week one of my challenge: an advent calendar you can make for someone, or a whole group, in a couple of minutes." |
| 2 | 4-12 s | Screen recording of the builder, sped up: write a few messages, pick dates, pick a theme | "One message per day" | "You write a message for each day, pick the dates and a theme. No account, no signup." |
| 3 | 12-20 s | Tap Share, paste into a chat, cut to a second phone | "One link" | "Then you send one link. One person or a group chat, your choice." |
| 4 | 20-30 s | Recipient phone: grid of doors, future doors greyed with their dates, today's opens | "A new door each day" | "They get a door that opens each day. Future ones stay locked until their date." |
| 5 | 30-40 s | Face to camera | "The honest part" | "The messages are encrypted on your phone before they're stored, so I can't read them. And you can cheat by changing your phone's date. Please don't." |
| 6 | 40-45 s | End card: palette, handle, site address | "Week 2: Dec 8" | "Free to use, link in my bio. Next week: something for the Secret Santa people." |

Only say "Secret Santa" if you're happy to reveal week 2 by then. Otherwise say "next week is another one".

## Post 2: the process (Thu Dec 3), about 45 seconds

Pick ONE real thing from the build and tell it as: problem, cause, fix. Do not invent one. Candidates to watch for while building:

- The unlock moment being off for someone in another timezone.
- A link that breaks when pasted into WhatsApp.
- A test that failed on the first try.
- A prompt that gave the wrong thing, next to what you changed.

Skeleton:

| # | Time | Picture | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0-4 s | The bug on screen | "This almost broke my advent calendar." (use your real one) |
| 2 | 4-15 s | Screen recording, sped up, of the problem happening | What it did and why it mattered |
| 3 | 15-30 s | The prompt or the code change | What caused it and what you changed |
| 4 | 30-40 s | The fix working, or the test passing | "Here it is working." |
| 5 | 40-45 s | Face or end card | "Next one is Tuesday." |

## Done when

Post 1: the door opens within 3 seconds, it's under 50 seconds, captions work with the sound off, and the address in your bio opens the app. Post 2: it tells one real story with a clear fix.
